# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Computer Science students use Tutorial Clinic to find study sessions, RSVP, track attendance, and access shared notes. Senior students and contributors use it to share resources, while society officers use it to support session logistics and moderation.

## Product Purpose

Tutorial Clinic is a mobile-first web app for the Computer Science Society's peer-supported study program. It centralizes events, QR-based RSVP and attendance, notes sharing, contribution points, and leaderboard motivation so students can study and participate more efficiently.

## Brand Personality

Focused, supportive, and student-friendly. The interface should feel calm enough for repeated academic use while still carrying the warmth of a campus community tool.

## Anti-references

Avoid feeling like a full learning management system, a marketing landing page, or a visually noisy gamified app. The product should not bury core study workflows under decorative UI.

## Design Principles

- Keep study and participation workflows direct.
- Make personal progress and contribution status visible without overwhelming the task.
- Use familiar product UI patterns so students can navigate quickly on mobile and desktop.
- Let community warmth come through restrained color, clear language, and useful context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Design for mobile web first and preserve accessible contrast, readable text sizes, visible focus states, and reduced-motion-friendly interactions. No personally sensitive information should be exposed beyond what the product requires.
